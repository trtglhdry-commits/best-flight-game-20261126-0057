An original 3D flight game that runs offline in a desktop browser. Fly a twin-engine courier jet through a mountain valley: thread the ring course, deliver parcels to five beacons across the map, and bring her home to runway 36. Throttle and turn discipline matter — full power drinks fuel, slow wings stall, and a hard touchdown is a crash. Chase and cockpit cameras, a full instrument HUD (airspeed, altitude, heading tape, artificial horizon, fuel), synthesised engine and wind audio, and a valley with a lake, a town, pine forests and snow-capped peaks.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or serve `game/` with any static server. No build step, no network requests, no downloads.

## Controls

- **↑ / ↓** pitch · **← / →** bank & turn · **Shift** throttle up · **Ctrl** throttle down · **Space** airbrake
- **Mouse:** hold & drag to steer · **C** camera (chase / cockpit) · **P** pause · **R** restart · **M** sound · **Esc** mission board
- **1 / 2 / 3** pick a mission from the menu, or click a mission card

## Missions

1. **Ring Run** — fly through all 8 rings before the clock runs out.
2. **Courier Run** — deliver to 5 beacons on one tank of fuel.
3. **Runway Landing** — short final at 500 ft; land gentle, slow and on the centreline.
